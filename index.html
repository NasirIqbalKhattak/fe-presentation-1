02 / TYPES OF DRUGS

Different drugs,
different effects.

Drugs affect the body in different ways. Legal does not always mean safe.

A↗

Stimulants

Speed up the brain and body.

Examples: cocaine, meth (“ice”)
B↘

Depressants

Slow the brain and body. Too much can stop breathing.

Examples: opioids, sleeping pills
C◎

Hallucinogens

Change what a person sees, feels, or thinks.

Examples: LSD
D◌

Cannabis

Can affect memory, focus, balance, and choices.

Risk depends on age and use

Remember: medicines can also harm us if used the wrong way.

Simple examples for learning. Source: UNODC.

05 / DRUGS ON CAMPUS

What about
college students?

College can bring new freedom. It can also bring stress, loneliness, and pressure.

01

Pressure can build up.
Exams, money worries, and fitting in can feel hard.

02

Fear can stop people speaking.
Worry about shame or punishment can delay help.

03

We do not have one reliable rate for all students.
Small studies do not show what is happening at every college in Pakistan.

Pakistan needs regular, private surveys that include students from many colleges.

Help, not blame.

LISTEN · STAY CALM · FIND HELP

Act fastIf someone cannot wake up or breathe well, call emergency help now.

09 / EVERYONE CAN HELP

We all have a part
to play.

Rules matter. So do prevention, care, and trust.

01

Drug control teams

  • Stop drug sellers and protect young people.
  • Work with schools and health services.
  • Share results and respect people's rights.
02

Universities

  • Make fair rules and private ways to ask for help.
  • Provide counseling and train staff.
  • Ask students about their needs without names.
03

Students and families

  • Share true facts and correct false ideas.
  • Care for one another; do not spread rumors.
  • Ask for help early.
MORE THAN ARRESTS.Prevent harm, offer care, and show results.
